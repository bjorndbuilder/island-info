Information for remote workers coming to Koh Lanta, Thailand

Chiang Mai to Koh Lanta

Last updated November 19, 2026

The quickest way from Chiang Mai to Koh Lanta is a direct flight to Krabi, then a transfer to the island. The flight takes about 2 hours and the transfer 2 to 2.5 hours, so count on 6-7 hours door to door.

Direct flights to Krabi #

  • Thai AirAsia flies Chiang Mai to Krabi direct two to three times a day. The morning flight gets you to Koh Lanta by early afternoon.
  • Bangkok Airways also lists a direct flight a few times a week in the winter season. Check the current schedule, as it has been suspended before.

Direct flights aren’t cheap: expect roughly 3,500-5,000 THB one way in high season, booked a few weeks ahead.

Other airlines (Thai Lion Air, Nok Air, Thai Airways, Thai Vietjet) connect through Bangkok, even when booking sites call the flight “direct”. A connection is often cheaper than the direct flight, but count on 7-9 hours or more door to door.

From Krabi Airport, take a shared minivan or private transfer to the island. See Krabi Airport to Koh Lanta for prices, the car ferry and what to do if you land late.

Flying to Phuket instead #

There are also direct flights from Chiang Mai to Phuket (Bangkok Airways, Thai AirAsia and Thai Vietjet, about 2 hours). From Phuket, a speedboat to Koh Lanta takes about 1.5 hours, with a reduced service from May to October. There are no direct flights from Chiang Mai to Trang.

Before you book, check the visa rules for Koh Lanta. For other routes, see getting to Koh Lanta.
