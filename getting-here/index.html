Getting to Koh Lanta

Last updated November 19, 2026

Most people get to Koh Lanta by flying to Krabi and taking a road transfer of about 2 hours. Koh Lanta has no airport, and there’s no road bridge from the mainland yet, so every route ends with either a car ferry or a boat.

Check the visa rules for Koh Lanta before you book: since September 2026 most nationalities get 30 days visa-free, not 60.

By ferry from other islands #

Boats from Phuket and Phi Phi run all year, with fewer sailings in low season (May to October). Speedboats from Ao Nang pause for part of the low season, and most island-hopping routes, like Koh Lipe or Koh Ngai, only run from about mid-October or November to April or May.

Operators include Bundhaya Speed Boat, Andaman Wave Master, Satun Pakbara SpeedBoat Club, Tigerline Ferry, and the slower Chaokoh and Lanta Petpailin ferries. Speedboats from Phuket leave from Rassada Pier, Tigerline from Chean Vanich Pier. Book through travel agencies in Saladan or online through Ferryhopper or 12Go Asia. In peak months (December/January), book at least a few days ahead as popular routes sell out.

All ferries arrive at Saladan Pier on the northern tip of the island.

Good to know #

  • The car ferry from Hua Hin pier to Koh Lanta Noi is the only road connection to the mainland. It runs from about 6am to 10pm, so plan ahead if you land late. A bridge from the mainland is planned to open in 2029.
  • Coming from Phuket by road rather than by boat takes about 5 hours or more.
  • Travel agencies all over Saladan sell combined tickets for any route. Prices are similar everywhere.
