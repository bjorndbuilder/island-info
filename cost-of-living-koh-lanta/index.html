Cost of living on Koh Lanta

Last updated November 19, 2026

The cost of living on Koh Lanta depends mostly on your rent and the season. A simple month with a bungalow, local food and a scooter comes to roughly 25,000-35,000 THB. With a nicer place in high season, a coworking membership and more meals out, expect 45,000-60,000 THB.

Monthly rents are the long-stay rates you get when you negotiate directly. Booking sites and nightly rates are higher. Most other prices come from the pages on this site: accommodation, coworking, transportation, gyms, internet and SIM cards, money and visa.

Two example months #

These are our estimates, added up from the prices above.

A simple month: about 25,000-35,000 THB #

  • Air-conditioned bungalow: 10,000-15,000 THB
  • Mostly local food, around 300 THB a day: about 9,000 THB
  • Scooter: 3,000-5,000 THB
  • SIM card: about 1,200 THB
  • Working from cafes, plus a few beers, massages and laundry: a few thousand THB

A comfortable month in high season: about 45,000-60,000 THB #

  • A nicer bungalow or studio in December to February: 20,000 THB or more
  • Eating out more, around 500 THB a day: about 15,000 THB
  • Coworking membership: 6,900 THB
  • Scooter and SIM card: about 5,000 THB
  • Going out, massages, trips: 8,000 THB or more

If you’d rather not arrange anything yourself, KoHub’s coliving package covers your room, coworking and two meals a day for 49,500-89,500 THB a month, depending on the month.

How the season changes the price #

Rent is what changes most. December to February is the most expensive time, and the best places fill up months ahead. In low season (May to October), the places that stay open are often 30-50% cheaper, but most of the island closes. See the best time to visit Koh Lanta for what each season is like.

Ways to spend less #

  • Negotiate a monthly rate directly, in person or over WhatsApp. It’s usually much cheaper than a booking site.
  • Rent your scooter by the month instead of by the day.
  • Eat at local Thai restaurants away from the beach road.
  • Every ATM withdrawal costs 250 THB, so take out as much as you can at once. Krungsri’s yellow ATMs let you withdraw the most per transaction (see money).
  • Work from cafes with good WiFi instead of paying for coworking every day.

Sources for prices not on our other pages: monthly rents from Expats Thai and Rentals in Thailand, food and beer from In Koh Lanta and Numbeo, massage from Massage Prices, laundry from Thaizer. Coworking prices are from KoHub (November 2026).
