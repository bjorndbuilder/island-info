Best coffee

Last updated November 19, 2026

Where to find the best coffee on Koh Lanta, and where to buy beans, decaf and equipment to make your own. Looking for a cafe with good WiFi to work from? See the cafes on the coworking page.

Cafes #

Slow Bar Caffetaria - the best coffee on the island. Irregular opening hours, so check before you go.

The Wild Brew - Small coffee shop with a perfect 5.0 on Google from more than 200 reviews (November 2026). Reviewers single out the coffee beans and the owner, who takes time for every customer.

Mem’s Coffee Corner - On the road to Lanta Animal Welfare, and one of the best-reviewed cafes on the island (4.9 from more than 900 reviews). Good coffee and breakfast.

Peak Café - Popular for its iced coffee drinks, with a big menu (4.7 from more than 850 reviews).

Beans #

Slow Bar Caffetaria - also sells beans

Oasis Yoga - sells beans in their shop

Bonnie’s Deli - sells beans and ground coffee

Lanta Mart - sells beans and ground coffee

Decaf #

You can buy ground decaf coffee online on https://th.iherb.com?ref=lantanomads.com.

You can order a decaf americano in KoHub.

Equipment #

FULL-FILL Café, a vegetarian cafe and refill store in Klong Nin, sells simple french press cafetières.
