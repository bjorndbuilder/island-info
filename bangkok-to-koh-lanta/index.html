Information for remote workers coming to Koh Lanta, Thailand

Bangkok to Koh Lanta

Last updated November 19, 2026

The fastest way from Bangkok to Koh Lanta is a flight to Krabi and a transfer to the island. Count on 5-6 hours door to door with a shared minivan, or 4-5 hours with a private car. There’s no airport on Koh Lanta.

Fly to Krabi (the easy option) #

Flights take about 1.5 hours and there are dozens a day from both Bangkok airports:

  • Suvarnabhumi (BKK): Bangkok Airways, Thai AirAsia, Thai Airways and Thai Vietjet
  • Don Mueang (DMK): Thai AirAsia, Nok Air and Thai Lion Air

Booked a few weeks ahead, one-way fares on the budget airlines usually run about 1,200-2,000 THB, with checked bags extra. Thai Airways and Bangkok Airways include a bag but cost more. Book only a few days ahead and you can pay double. From Krabi Airport it’s about 2 hours by car to the island, plus the car ferry. See Krabi Airport to Koh Lanta for minivans, private transfers and what to do if you land late.

Krabi or Phuket? #

Krabi, for most people. It’s the closest airport and the transfer works all year.

Phuket makes sense if you find a much better flight. From Rassada Pier in Phuket, a speedboat to Koh Lanta takes about 1.5 hours and costs about 900-1,500 THB. Boats run a reduced service from May to October, so check before you book. By road, Phuket to Koh Lanta takes about 5 hours or more.

Fly to Trang #

Trang is the other nearby airport, with flights from Don Mueang only (Thai AirAsia and Thai Lion Air). The minivan from Trang to Koh Lanta takes 2.5-3 hours and costs about 400-550 THB, but there are only a few departures around midday. It only works with a morning flight.

Night bus #

The cheapest option, but you lose a night and most of a day:

  • Through ticket: buses leave from the Khao San Road area around 19:00 and you arrive at Saladan the next morning, about 16.5 hours later. You switch from the bus to a minivan in Krabi. Around 1,300-1,550 THB.
  • Two tickets: a bus from the Southern Bus Terminal to Krabi (about 11 hours), then a minivan to Koh Lanta (2.5-3 hours).

Overnight train #

There’s no railway to Krabi or Koh Lanta, so the train goes to Trang. Trains leave from Krung Thep Aphiwat station (Bang Sue), not Hua Lamphong:

  • Train 83: leaves 18:50, arrives in Trang around 08:15 (about 13.5 hours)
  • Train 167: leaves 20:30, arrives in Trang around 11:00 (about 14.5 hours)

A second-class air-conditioned sleeper berth costs about 720-830 THB. From Trang, take the minivan to Koh Lanta (2.5-3 hours). It’s the comfortable way to go overland, but door to door it takes 17-19 hours.

Before you book, check the visa rules for Koh Lanta. For other routes, see getting to Koh Lanta.
