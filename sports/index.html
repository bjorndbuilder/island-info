Information for remote workers coming to Koh Lanta, Thailand

Gyms and fitness

Last updated November 19, 2026

Koh Lanta has two air-conditioned gyms, WM Fitness in the Long Beach area and Luke’s Gym in Klong Khong. Both charge 200 THB for a day pass. There’s also a beach gym that’s effectively free if you eat there, plus a few group fitness classes.

Gyms #

WM Fitness #

WM Fitness - Long Beach area. Modern gym with good selection of cardio equipment, resistance machines, and free weights. Also has a rig area with battle ropes, sandbags, kettlebells. Connected to William Muay Thai Stadium. Air conditioned. Day pass 200 THB. See Facebook for full pricing information.

Luke’s Gym #

Luke’s Gym - European style gym in Klong Khong, surrounded by jungle. Dumbbells up to 50kg, many machines. Very clean with separate changing rooms and showers. Towels provided. Air conditioned. Protein shakes available (vegetarian/vegan options, 120 THB). Day pass 200 THB, see lukesgym.com for more pricing information. You need a scooter to get there.

Thai CAT Sport Center #

Thai CAT Sport Center - Fan-cooled gym near the beach where you can do a full workout with a bit of creativity. Entry is 100 THB per person, and you get a 100 THB voucher to spend on food or drinks at their beach restaurant, so the workout is free if you eat or drink there. A nice routine is to train first, then use the voucher on a Pad Thai and cool off in the sea while your lunch is prepared.

Equipment (March 2026):

  • Plates (kg): 2x15, 4x10, 4x5, 6x2.5, 2x1.25
  • Dumbbells (lbs): 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 50
  • 2 movable seats for dumbbell exercises
  • EZ bar
  • Leg press machine (you might need all the plates in the gym)
  • Smith machine with bench
  • Squat rack
  • Lat pulldown / seated row
  • Adjustable cables
  • Pull up / chin up
  • Dips
  • Abs roller
  • Cycle hometrainer

Muay Thai #

Koh Lanta has several Muay Thai gyms for every level, from first-timers to people preparing for a fight. Most run group classes in the morning and late afternoon.

Lanta Muay Thai Complex #

Lanta Muay Thai Complex - Near Klong Dao. Beginner, intermediate/advanced and kids’ classes, plus private lessons and sparring. Group classes come as packages: 1,800 THB for 5 sessions, 2,200 THB for a week, 7,500 THB for a month (one session a day). Open daily 8am to 7:30pm. See lantamuaythaicomplex.com for all prices.

Lanta Muay Thai Academy #

Lanta Muay Thai Academy - Long Beach area, and the gym with the most Google reviews on the island. Check their Instagram for current class times and prices.

Rainbow Muay Thai #

Rainbow Muay Thai - Klong Dao area, with near-perfect Google reviews. No website, so call them on 094 517 2021 for times and prices.

Classes #

Fit On Lanta - Group fitness classes in the morning and evening. Classes include HIIT, strength training, yoga, and more. Suitable for all fitness levels. See website for class schedule and pricing information.

Slava Total Workout - High intensity functional training class, held every weekday at 8 in the morning at the Lanta Sports Restort. Also at 9:30 in the morning on Monday, Wednesday and Fridays. 60 minutes of bodyweight exercises, kettlebells, battle ropes, and sandbags. Suitable for all fitness levels. See Facebook for schedule and pricing.

Yoga #

Oasis Yoga #

Oasis Yoga - Yoga in a tree house studio at Oasis Yoga Bungalows on Klong Dao. Classes every morning, plus afternoon classes on some days: Vinyasa flow, yin and sunset stretch. Non-guests are welcome. There are no reservations, so just turn up (check-in opens 35 minutes before class). Mats are provided. A class costs 400 THB, 10 classes 3,000 THB, or 4,500 THB for a monthly unlimited pass, and meditation is free. The schedule changes every month, see oasisyogabungalows.com.

Lanta Yoga #

Lanta Yoga - Small studio in the Long Beach area. Vinyasa flow on Monday, Wednesday and Friday from 9 to 10:30am. 300 THB per class or 2,500 THB for 10 classes. See lanta-yoga.com for the current schedule.
