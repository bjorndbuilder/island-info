Where to stay

Last updated November 19, 2026

Most nomads on Koh Lanta stay in a bungalow or small resort around Long Beach (Phra Ae) since it’s close to KoHub. Prices vary a lot by season. High season (Dec-Feb) books up fast and costs roughly double what you’d pay in shoulder months (Nov, Mar-Apr). If you’re coming in peak season, book ahead. Outside of that, you can often just show up and walk around.

Some places are cheaper when you message them directly on WhatsApp or Facebook. Monthly rates are almost always negotiable in person.

KoHub Bungalows #

The obvious pick if you want the full coliving/coworking setup. Private bungalows (28 sqm) right next to the KoHub coworking space, about a 5-minute walk from the beach. Each bungalow has AC, king bed, hot shower, mini-fridge, desk, office chair and 1 Gbps WiFi with its own access point.

Packages are all-inclusive (room + coworking + two meals/day):

  • November and April: 49,500 THB/month
  • December and March: 69,500 THB/month
  • January and February: 89,500 THB/month
  • Closed May to October.

Prices are from KoHub’s packages page for the 2026-27 season.

The community aspect is the main draw. Weekly events, communal lunches, island trips. It’s not the cheapest option, but you’re paying for convenience and not having to figure everything out yourself.

The preferred way to get availability and book is by email. The address is on KoHub.org.

Lanta Buddy Resort #

Literally next to Kohub. And offers a different kind of convenience with a kitchentte that has a bigger fridge, a kettle and a microwave. Garden views, terrace, quiet setting.

Book on Booking.com or WhatsApp

Morakot Resort #

Solid mid-range option with a pool. Rated 8.8 on Booking.com with especially high marks for value (9.0). Staff are friendly, rooms are clean, and they have daily housekeeping. Book on Booking.com - it’s cheaper than contacting them directly.

Lanta Grand House #

Budget pick with great staff. 14 rooms, all with AC. About a 5-10 minute walk from Long Beach. Has a small restaurant and cafe on-site. Rooms from around 400-900 THB/night. The owner goes out of his way to help with tours, transport and anything else you need. Good value if you don’t need a pool or beachfront.

Cheeky Monkey / Anna House #

Nice bungalows with kitchen. Across the road from Kohub. Cheapest way to book is to message them on WhatsApp. Not much of an online presence, so you won’t find many reviews. Ask in the nomad community for recent experiences.

Tips for booking #

  • High season (December to February): Prices are at their highest and the best places fill up months ahead. For a stay in December or January, book by October or November. See the best time to visit Koh Lanta for what each season is like.
  • Shoulder season (November, March and April): Easier to find a place and cheaper. A few days ahead is usually enough.
  • Low season (May to October): Some places close, but plenty stay open, especially around Long Beach and Klong Dao, often at 30-50% less. Check that a place is actually open before you book, as some show up on booking sites while they’re closed.
  • Monthly rates: Most places will do a monthly rate if you ask, especially for four weeks or more. That usually works in person or over WhatsApp, not through booking sites.
  • Booking sites: Compare Agoda and Booking.com. Prices differ, and some smaller places are only on one of them.
  • Walk-ins: Outside high season, walking in and asking can get you a better price than online. In high season it’s risky, since the good places are full.
